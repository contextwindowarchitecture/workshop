You can export a single project or the whole workspace. Exports include tasks, subtasks, comments, labels and assignees. Attachments are exported separately as a ZIP file.

You can export one project or the entire workspace. Exports include tasks, subtasks, comments, labels and assignees, and attachments are exported separately as a ZIP archive.

Exports are prepared in the background. For a large workspace the email with the download link usually arrives within an hour, and the link works for 24 hours.

If an export fails, retry it once; if it fails again, contact support@fernway.example with the workspace name and the time of the attempt.
