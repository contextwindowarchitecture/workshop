A project groups related tasks. Each project has three views of the same tasks: Board (columns you drag cards between), List (a sortable table), and Timeline (tasks with start and due dates on a calendar). Switching views never changes the tasks themselves.

Board columns are your project's workflow stages, such as To do, In progress and Done. Rename, reorder or add columns from the column menu. Moving a card to the last column marks the task complete.

Archive a finished project from its menu with Archive project. Archived projects are read-only and hidden from the sidebar, but still searchable and still count toward storage. Restore one from Settings, then Archived projects.

On the Free plan a workspace can have 3 active projects. Archived projects do not count toward that limit. Paid plans have unlimited projects.
