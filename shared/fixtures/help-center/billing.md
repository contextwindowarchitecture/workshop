Workspace owners and billing admins manage billing under Settings, then Billing. Fernway charges per member: every member counts as a seat, guests do not.

Fernway accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards. Business workspaces on annual billing can also pay by invoice with 30-day payment terms; contact sales to switch.

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on every charge and are listed under Billing, then Invoices, as PDF downloads. To add a VAT or tax ID, edit Billing details; it appears on every invoice issued after the change.

When you add members mid-cycle, Fernway charges a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period. When you remove members, the unused time becomes a credit on your next invoice, not a refund.

You can change plans at any time. An upgrade takes effect immediately and is prorated. A downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
