title: Refunds and cancellation
version: 4
updated: 2026-03-30T09:00:00Z
---
Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of the first payment or of a renewal. The refund is prorated for the unused months and returns to the original payment method within 10 business days.

Monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time. Cancellation stops the next renewal; the workspace keeps its paid features until the end of the period you have paid for, then moves to the Free plan.

To cancel, the workspace owner opens Settings, then Billing, and chooses Cancel plan. To request a refund on an annual plan, contact support from the same page within the 30-day window.
