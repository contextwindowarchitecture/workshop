Every workspace member has one role: Owner, Admin, Member or Guest. A workspace has at least one Owner. Only Owners can delete the workspace, change the plan, or transfer ownership.

Admins manage members, projects and integrations, and can see every project in the workspace, including private ones. Admins cannot change billing unless an Owner also makes them a billing admin.

Members create projects and tasks, and see every public project plus the private projects they are added to. Guests see only the projects they are invited to and cannot create projects.

Change a member's role under Settings, then Members, from the role menu next to their name. Role changes take effect immediately.
