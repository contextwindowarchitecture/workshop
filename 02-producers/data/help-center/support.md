Free workspaces get help in the community forum at community.fernway.example, where the Fernway team answers questions on weekdays.

Team and Business workspaces can email support@fernway.example or use the Help menu in the app. Team requests get a first reply within one business day; Business requests get a first reply within 4 hours, around the clock.

Include your workspace URL and, for a problem with a task, its key such as FW-123. Support never asks for your password or your API tokens.
