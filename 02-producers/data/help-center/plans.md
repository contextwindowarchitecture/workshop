Fernway has three plans: Free, Team and Business. Every plan includes unlimited tasks, the Board, List and Timeline views, and the desktop and mobile apps.

The Free plan is for up to 5 members, with 3 active projects, 1 GB of file storage and 7 days of activity history. Support is through the community forum.

The Team plan costs $9 per member per month billed annually, or $11 billed monthly. It adds unlimited projects, 100 GB of storage, guests, recurring tasks, the Slack and GitHub integrations, and email support that replies within one business day.

The Business plan costs $19 per member per month billed annually, or $23 billed monthly. It includes everything in Team, plus SAML single sign-on, SCIM provisioning, an audit log, a 99.9% uptime SLA, data residency in the EU or the US, 1 TB of storage, and priority support that replies within 4 hours.

Single sign-on and SCIM are not available on the Team plan. A Team workspace that needs them must upgrade to Business.
