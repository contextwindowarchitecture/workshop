A scrip issue, split or consolidation changes the number of units held. The custodian applies it to the ledger on the settlement date; until then the ledger shows the pre-action units, and the market-data vendor may already show the post-action estimate.

The ledger is the record. When the portal and a vendor estimate differ, the ledger figure is reported and the pending action is mentioned with its expected settlement date.

Dividends and interest are credited to cash on the payment date and appear on the ledger the same day.
