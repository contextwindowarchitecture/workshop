Market orders placed during exchange hours are sent immediately and settle two business days after the trade date. An order queued through the assistant is not placed until the client confirms it in the portal; the confirmation shows the symbol, side, quantity and the estimated value at the last quote.

A sell order cannot exceed the settled units the ledger holds for the account. Units from a corporate action count only once the custodian has settled them and they appear on the ledger.

Prices shown in the portal are the vendor's last quote and may be delayed by up to fifteen minutes. Valuations are estimates until settlement.
