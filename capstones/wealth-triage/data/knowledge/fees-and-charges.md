Advisory accounts pay an annual advisory fee of 0.75% of assets, charged quarterly in arrears. Self-directed accounts pay no advisory fee and a dealing charge of 7.50 per order.

Custody is charged at 0.10% a year on all accounts. There is no charge for holding cash, and no exit fee on transfers out of the platform.

Fees are shown on the quarterly statement and in the account's Charges page, with the period each fee covers.
