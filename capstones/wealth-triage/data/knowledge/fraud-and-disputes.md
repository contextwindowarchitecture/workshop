title: Suspected fraud and disputed transactions (fictional teaching material)
version: 5
updated: 2026-06-10T09:00:00Z
---
A client who reports a transfer or payment they did not make is treated as a suspected fraud report. Open a case for the fraud team at high urgency, and stop any pending outbound transfer on the account until the team has spoken to the client.

A report from a device the account has not used before, or within 24 hours of a credential change, is escalated to critical urgency.

A transaction the client recognises but disagrees with (an amount, a fee, a duplicate) is a dispute for the disputes team at normal urgency. Being locked out of the portal is an access request at normal urgency.

The assistant does not move money, freeze cards or confirm the fraud; it opens the case and tells the client what happens next.
