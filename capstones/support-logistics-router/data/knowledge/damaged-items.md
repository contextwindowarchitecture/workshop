If an item arrives damaged or defective, report it within 14 days of delivery from the order page with Report a problem, and attach two photos: the item and the outer packaging.

For damaged items we send a replacement first, free of charge, and include a prepaid label for the damaged one. You do not need to wait for the return to arrive before the replacement ships.

If no replacement is in stock, we refund the item and its share of shipping in full within 5 business days of your report being approved.

Damage reported more than 14 days after delivery is handled under the manufacturer's warranty; we will give you the manufacturer's contact details and your proof of purchase.
