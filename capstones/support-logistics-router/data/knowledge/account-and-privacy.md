Our support team can see your orders, your delivery address and your contact details, and never your full card number.

Support agents will never ask for your password, a one-time code or your full card number. If someone does, end the conversation and contact us from the website.

You can download or delete your account data from Account, then Privacy. Deletion takes up to 30 days and cancels open returns.
