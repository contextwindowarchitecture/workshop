Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over 50. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

Every shipment has a tracking link in your order page and in the dispatch email. Tracking updates can lag the carrier by up to 12 hours.

A parcel that shows as delivered but has not arrived is usually with a neighbour or at a safe place; please check for 24 hours. After that, report it from the order page and we open a carrier investigation, which takes up to 5 business days.

We ship to the EU and the UK. Duties and taxes for UK orders are included at checkout.
