Refunds go back to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card or to a bank account.

To exchange a size or colour, start a return for the original and place a new order; the new order ships immediately and the return is refunded when it arrives. Exchanges are not held in stock.

Refunds are issued within 5 business days of the returned parcel arriving at our warehouse, and your bank may take a further 3 to 5 business days to show it.

Shipping charges are refunded only when the return is our fault: a damaged, defective or wrong item.
