Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with tags attached.

Final-sale items, gift cards and personalised products cannot be returned. Footwear can be returned if it has only been tried on indoors.

To start a return, open the order in your account and choose Return items. We email a prepaid label within minutes; drop the parcel at any carrier point within 14 days of requesting the return.

Refunds are issued within 5 business days of the returned parcel arriving at our warehouse, and your bank may take a further 3 to 5 business days to show it.
