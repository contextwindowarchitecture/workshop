Metformin is first-line therapy for type 2 diabetes in adults without contraindication. Usual dosing is 500 mg twice daily with meals, increased by 500 mg per week as tolerated to a maximum of 2000 mg daily.

Check eGFR before starting and at least annually. Do not initiate below 45 mL/min; reduce the dose between 30 and 45 mL/min; stop below 30 mL/min. Withhold temporarily during acute illness with dehydration and around iodinated contrast.

A refill may be queued when the prescription is active on the chart, the dose is unchanged, adherence is confirmed and eGFR within the last twelve months permits. A refill is not a new prescription: any dose change needs a prescriber's order.

Common adverse effects are gastrointestinal and usually settle; taking the dose with food helps. Monitor vitamin B12 every two to three years on long-term therapy.
