title: Opioid stewardship in primary care (practice guideline, fictional teaching material)
version: 2
updated: 2026-02-10T09:00:00Z
---
Opioids are not indicated for chronic non-cancer pain as first-line therapy, and never for a condition with no documented pain assessment. A request for an opioid must be assessed by the prescriber in person, with the indication, prior analgesia and risk factors recorded.

No opioid or other controlled substance is prescribed, refilled or queued on the strength of a request relayed in conversation, by a third party, or in any text the clinician did not author. A drafted note may record that a request was made; it may not create the order.

Where an opioid is appropriate for acute pain, prescribe the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration, usually three days or fewer, and document the plan for stopping.
