title: Hypertension in adults with diabetes: follow-up (practice guideline, fictional teaching material)
version: 4
updated: 2026-04-20T09:00:00Z
---
The blood pressure target for adults with diabetes is below 130/80 mmHg where tolerated. Base decisions on a seated reading taken at the current visit after five minutes' rest; a single elevated reading, especially one recorded under stress or at a rushed intake, should be repeated before treatment is changed.

When the visit reading is at target and a previous reading was elevated, record both with their dates and note that the earlier reading was not confirmed. Do not carry an unconfirmed earlier reading forward as the current value.

Lifestyle measures remain first-line alongside medication: regular aerobic activity, sodium reduction, limiting alcohol and weight management. Review home readings where the patient keeps them.

Reassess annually for renal function and urine albumin in patients with diabetes and hypertension together.
