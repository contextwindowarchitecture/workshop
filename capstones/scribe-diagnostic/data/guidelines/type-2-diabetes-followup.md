title: Type 2 diabetes: routine follow-up (practice guideline, fictional teaching material)
version: 3
updated: 2026-06-01T09:00:00Z
---
At each routine follow-up, review HbA1c, blood pressure, weight, medication adherence and side effects, and ask about symptoms of hyperglycaemia such as polyuria, polydipsia and fatigue. Record the measured blood pressure from the visit, not a value carried forward from a previous note.

The HbA1c target for most adults is below 7.0%. An HbA1c between 7.0% and 7.9% on metformin alone warrants a review of diet, activity and adherence before treatment is intensified; repeat HbA1c in three months. Above 8.0%, or with symptoms, intensify treatment at the visit.

Continue metformin where tolerated and renal function allows. A refill of a stable metformin prescription is appropriate at a follow-up visit when adherence is confirmed and the most recent eGFR is above 45 mL/min.

Annual checks: foot examination, retinal screening, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, lipids and renal function. Document the date each was last performed and schedule any that are overdue.
